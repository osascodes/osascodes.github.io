About

Systems first. UI that ships.

Solomon Kayode is a Computer Science graduate who builds full-stack products with Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind, and shadcn/ui on the client — Supabase, Prisma, and Postgres underneath. Auth, storage, and row-level security are in the first cut.

Recent work is operational software for shops that run from a phone: inventory, sales, customers, debt, expenses, staff, a mini storefront, and receipts. The bar is simple — mobile first, production ready, fast enough to open between customers.
