About

I ship the interface and the system behind it.

I'm Benjamin Osahon, a full-stack developer in Lagos. I build websites, stores, and business tools for owners who need something that works on a phone.

I work across the stack — React and Next.js on the front, Node, Supabase, MongoDB, and MySQL on the back. One person can take a brief from layout to database to deploy.

I'm also building BizFlow, a mobile-first business OS for vendors — inventory, sales, receipts, customers, and WhatsApp checkout. Same stack I use on client work.

Business administration is the other half. I care about checkout, stock, and whether the thing actually helps the business run.
