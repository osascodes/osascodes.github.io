About

I ship the interface and the system behind it

I'm Benjamin Osahon, a full-stack developer in Lagos, Nigeria. I don't just write code — I design and build clean, intentional websites, online stores, and web applications that are easy to use, responsive, and built around the people using them.

I take projects from idea to deployment, handling the user experience, frontend, backend, database, and everything in between. I work with React and Next.js on the frontend, and Node.js, Supabase, MongoDB, and MySQL on the backend.

Right now, I'm also building BizFlow, a mobile-first business platform for vendors to manage inventory, sales, receipts, and customers. Building a real product keeps me focused on more than just making things work — I pay attention to the experience, the details, and how people actually use what I build.

Business Administration is the other half. It gives me a business perspective on what I build — I care about the user experience, the problem being solved, and whether the final product actually makes sense for the business
