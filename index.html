About

I ship the interface and the system behind it.

I'm Benjamin Osahon, a full-stack developer in Lagos Nigeria. I build websites, stores, and business tools for owners who need something that works on a phone, not a slide deck.

I work across the stack — React and Next.js on the front, Node, Supabase, MongoDB, and MySQL on the back. One person can take a brief from layout to database to deploy.

Right now I'm also building BizFlow, a mobile-first business OS for vendors — inventory, sales, receipts, customers, and WhatsApp checkout. That product work is how I stay sharp on the same stack I use for client gigs.

Business administration is the other half. I care about checkout, stock, customers, and whether the thing actually helps the business run.
